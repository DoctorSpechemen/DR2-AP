# Dead Rising 2

## What does randomization do to this game?

Finishing cases, beating psychopaths, bringing survivors back to the safe house, reaching zombie kill milestones,
levelling up and finding Zombrex all send checks. Cash, PP, Zombrex and traps arrive from other worlds. With **Case Gate
Items** on, each case from Case 2 onwards needs its own item before it opens.

## What is the goal?

Finish **The Facts**, or **Overtime** if you choose that goal.

## What does another world's item look like in Dead Rising 2?

A message box at the top of the screen with a short chime tells you what you found and who it is for.
